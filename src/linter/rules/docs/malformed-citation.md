Warns about incomplete citation markers, such as `[@]`, `[@ key]`, an empty
`@{}`, or a missing closing bracket. Write a key immediately after `@`, and
close every bracket or brace. Use `@{...}` for keys with unusual characters.

The rule skips code, math, escaped markers, email addresses, and working links.
To check whether a complete key exists in the bibliography, use
[`citation-keys`](#citation-keys).
